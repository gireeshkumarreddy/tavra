    02 Craft

    Every piece starts with a craft that already exists: bandhani, ikat and hand-block print, redrawn for performance knits and finished by hand.

    • BandhaniTie-dye from Kutch, reborn as print
    • Pochampally ikatChevrons for jacquard side panels

    03 About

    About TĀVRA

    About TĀVRA

    Rooted in India, built to move

    TĀVRA is a women's sportswear label. We take India's textile art, from Pichwai lotus ponds to Kalamkari paisley, and place it on Western performance shapes: racer bras, high-rise leggings, track jackets. Indian at heart, made for training, travel and everyday life.

    AWhy we play

    The game doesn’t start at the score.

    It starts in the hours nobody films: the early laps, the footwork drills, the last free throw after practice. TĀVRA kit is cut for those hours.

    Every piece pairs a Western performance cut with one Indian print, placed where the body moves and kept off the seams that rub.
