Project

Pichwai lotus ponds, redrawn as a single stem that runs down the outer seam of a forest-green legging. Botanical rhythm for the studio and the street.

Tiger and jungle illustrations move onto the sleeves of a navy track jacket. The body stays clean and athletic, and the artwork carries the story.

Lotus stems, paisley and block-print geometry become considered placements on contemporary athletic shapes. Original artwork with Indian roots.
